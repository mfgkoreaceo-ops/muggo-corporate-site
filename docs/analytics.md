## Implementation

`scripts/analytics.js` loads gtag.js only after the visitor grants analytics consent. No Google script or events load before consent. The ID/EN/KO banner offers equal allow/decline choices. The footer settings button can withdraw consent and clear GA cookies. Advertising consent remains denied. No user IDs, form fields, application content, arbitrary query strings, article identifiers, or raw referrer paths are sent by the integration.

Events: `page_view` for logical route changes, `language_select` for language changes, `order_click` for links to order.muggo.co.id. An order click is not a completed order or purchase. Article detail pages are aggregated. Route rerenders and language rerenders do not duplicate pageviews.

Enhanced Measurement is OFF in the web stream: leave it off to avoid duplicate history pageviews and unsolicited form/search/link collection. `send_page_view: false` disables the config event. Session/engagement events generated by GA remain possible after consent.

UTM source/medium/campaign/content are accepted only as campaign slugs (ASCII letters, digits, underscore, hyphen; start with a letter; at most 80 characters). Never put personal information in campaign slugs. UTM term, arbitrary parameters and click IDs are omitted. Referrers are reduced to their origin. Custom event parameters are available in DebugView; custom dimensions may be registered later for long-term breakdowns.

Production collection is restricted to muggo.co.id and www.muggo.co.id. Local/preview hosts, /admin paths, and URLs containing cms_preview, test_user or analytics_off are excluded. For manual internal QA open `https://muggo.co.id/?analytics_off=1`. GA cannot retrospectively recover visits from before installation; consent rejection and blockers reduce measured visits.

## Disable / rollback

Set `ga4MeasurementId` to an empty string in site.config.js and redeploy to disable collection and the consent UI. Vercel Web Analytics remains disabled.
